Fonts embedded in The XaLZa
============================

- Space Grotesk (Regular, SemiBold) — designed by Florian Karsten
- Bebas Neue (Regular) — designed by Ryoichi Tsunekawa, Dharma Type
- Space Mono (Regular, Bold) — designed by Colophon Foundry / Google Fonts

Bebas Neue and Space Mono are part of the Xazainspain brand's own type
system (see the brand's GUIA-DE-MARCA.md): Bebas Neue for headline/title
text, Space Mono for technical labels and data readouts (dB, Hz, ms, LUFS
throughout the plugin) — replacing the previous IBM Plex Mono.

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). The OFL permits embedding these fonts in a
software binary (including a commercial VST3 plugin) and redistributing
them as part of that software, at no cost and without additional
permission, provided the fonts themselves are not sold on their own.
